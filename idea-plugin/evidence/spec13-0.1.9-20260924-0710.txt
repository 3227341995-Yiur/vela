WHAT THIS FILE IS
=================

`SPEC.md` §13 landed in the compiler while the 0.1.8 round was being measured: `enum` and `match`
became reserved words, payload variants and `match` became syntax, and eighteen corpus files
(`tests\build\enum_*.vel`, `tests\probes\enum_*`) arrived with them.  This is the round that makes
the plugin follow: the two keywords, the six PSI node kinds the compiler prints for §13, the model
and completion support that reads them, and every number in `FEATURE_PARITY.md` re-measured on the
result.

Three measurements are in here, and each is a *before and after* rather than an assertion:

  1. **The keyword axis, and its falsification.**  `PlatformEntry` row 7's `bare-end` family was
     **red** -- 139 files, one position each, every one reporting the same two missing keywords --
     and is green with the two words in `VelaTokenTypes.KEYWORDS`.  Removing them again makes it
     red on demand; the raw output of that mutation is below.
  2. **The tree axis, against the compiler's own printer.**  `ast-diff.ps1` and `psi-tree-diff.ps1`
     on the grown corpus, with the node and leaf counts.
  3. **`HoverTruth` went red first and is green again**, and the red is the interesting one: the
     plugin's hover was right and *the tool's oracle* knew only four declaration kinds.  Both the
     red and the fix are quoted below, and it is written up as Open-defects entry 9.

WHAT THE PLUGIN STILL DOES NOT DO FOR §13, NAMED
================================================

  * **A variant name is not resolved by go-to-declaration, find-usages or rename.**  `VelaTargets`
    resolves a call to a `def` and a bare name to a struct; a variant is neither.  `GotoOracle`
    counts those positions as `invisible-member` (10 → 18 with this corpus, printed in its log), and
    `RenameOracle` does not judge a variant at all, because its declaration walk enumerates
    `def`/`struct`/`field`/`param`/`decl`/`for`.  So a variant rename is **not claimed**.
  * **No popup for a variant construction.**  `Circle(2.0)` has no `def` to resolve, so
    `findElementForParameterInfo` answers null; `PlatformEntry` row 9 judges none of them and counts
    them in `callee-not-a-declared-def` (599 → 623).
  * **No semantic-highlight colour of its own for a variant name.**  `VelaSemanticHighlighting`
    classifies a name as a type or as a callable; a variant is neither (`Circle(2.0)` is a call and
    `Empty` is a bare name), and the decision to leave it to the default colour is written where it
    is made.  No measurement covers this, which is said rather than implied.
  * **A payload field is not a symbol.**  The model declares none -- nothing in the language can
    name one -- so `HoverTruth` counts 15 payload fields as
    `payload-field-not-a-model-declaration` instead of demanding a hover for them, and the structure
    view shows a variant with its payload spelling in its detail string rather than as child nodes.

WHAT THIS FILE DOES NOT PROVE
=============================

  * **No IDE was started.**  Every harness here drives the plugin's own entry points with stand-ins
    for the platform's editor, PSI and contexts; that is stated in each tool's own header.
  * The corpus was not frozen: `tests\` belongs to another track and is still growing, so the
    counts above are one run over the tree as it stood at 07:00-07:10, and the `enum_*` files
    themselves may gain more.
  * `SymbolDiff` and `FoldDiff` are failing by design and their numbers are the measured difference
    from a retired implementation, not defects in the current one; the paragraph above says what
    moved them and why.
  * The plugin's parser reproduces the compiler's **parse** refusals (a nested pattern, `enum` used
    as a name), not its **checker** refusals (arity, exhaustiveness, a duplicate arm, an unknown
    variant).  A file the checker refuses is a tree here and a diagnostic from `vm.exe check` in
    the editor, which is where the refusal is shown to the user.
